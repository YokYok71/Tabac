v1.0 · Build 25 Une pipe nettoyée puis refumée le jour même est de nouveau signalée août 2026

Correction importante

My Tobacco Cellar v1.0

v1.0 · Build 25 A pipe cleaned then smoked the same day is flagged again August 2026

Important fix

  • The cleaning reminder mishandled same-day order. A pipe cleaned and then smoked again within the day counted zero sessions since its cleaning and vanished from the reminders. Entries now carry a time, like sessions do: same-day order is exact in both directions.
  • An entry recorded before this version, or one whose field you clear, counts as though it happened at noon.

v1.0 · Build 1 First public release August 2026

New

  • My Tobacco Cellar 1.0. The app manages your pipe-tobacco cellar and your pipe collection: tobaccos and lots, pipes and their maintenance log, accessories, a tasting journal, statistics, a wishlist and a shopping list.
  • Everything stays on your device. Backing up to Google Drive or Dropbox is optional, and the JSON, CSV and ZIP exports are yours.
  • Six languages, light and dark mode, three palettes, and an adjustable text size.
